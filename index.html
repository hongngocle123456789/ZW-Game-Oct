The celebration was wonderful. Now the house needs some love! Clean up the party, prepare waste and sort every item into the right bin.

One house. Five sorting bins. 60 seconds.

HOW TO PLAY

Collect. Prepare. Sort.

Follow four simple steps. The game lasts 60 seconds.

1. PICK UP

Drag a labelled item from the messy house. Items are placed around the table, counter and floor. New items appear as you clean.

2. PREPARE

Drag dirty plastics to the sink, or cardboard to the table.

3. SORT

Drag each prepared item into its correct bin. Try again if needed.

4. FINISH

Sort as many items correctly as you can before time runs out.

DRAG an item to the sink or table if needed, then DRAG it to the correct bin. You can also tap an item and then tap its destination.

Five bins: landfill, organic, clean plastics, paper and aluminium cans. Item photographs load from Wikimedia Commons when online; illustrated fallbacks remain available offline.
